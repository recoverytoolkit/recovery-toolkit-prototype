How to Use Your Recovery Toolkit

When a craving hits or your mind is racing, everything can feel overwhelming. Writing things down outside your head breaks the panic loop. It helps you see that urges always peak and pass, thoughts are just thoughts, and you never have to act on autopilot.

The Surfer: Use the 90-second breathing timer to ride out acute cravings safely.

The ABCDE Log: Separate what happened from what you told yourself, then actively challenge that belief (D) and write a more useful one (E).

The CBA Matrix: Weigh the short-term relief of using against the long-term cost, and the short-term discomfort of not using against the long-term gain.

Values Check: Step back from the urge and ask whether acting on it moves you toward, or away from, the person you want to be.

If/Then: Decide your response to a high-risk situation in advance, so you're not deciding from scratch while under pressure.
