Thought Unpacker (ABCDE)

Dissect automatic triggers and ground your thoughts in reality.

Practical Example Scenario

A (Trigger): Finished an overwhelming project late at night.

B (Belief): "I worked so hard, I deserve a drink/vape to relax, or I won't sleep."

C (Consequence): Tight chest, intense urge, anxiety (8/10).

D (Reality Check): "Giving in will ruin my sleep quality and increase my anxiety tomorrow morning. I've relaxed without it before."

E (New Focus): "I will drink chamomile tea, take a warm shower, and let the urge wave pass."

Cost-Benefit Analysis (CBA) Matrix

Weigh short-term relief against long-term peace of mind.

Practical Decisional Balance Example

Pros of Giving In: Instant relief, temporary escape from boredom or stress.

Cons of Giving In: Guilt, broken streak, grogginess, increased anxiety tomorrow.

Pros of Stepping Back: Self-respect, pride, better morning clarity, long-term health.

Cons of Stepping Back: Temporary physical restlessness for 10-15 minutes while the wave subsides.
